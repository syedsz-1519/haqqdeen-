## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, noble, and serene atmosphere for a modern Islamic knowledge and personal mastery platform. The brand personality balances classical Islamic reverence with contemporary forward-momentum: intellectual, trustworthy, luminous, and refined.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalism** with **Modern Editorial** styling and precise, subtle geometric accents. Generous whitespace honors clarity of mind, while structured layouts project purpose and institutional credibility. 

Visual parameters strictly follow an aniconic standard:
- **Zero living being depictions**: No human faces, silhouettes, or animal photography.
- **Symbolic & Architectural Metaphors**: Replace figurative imagery with precision geometric patterns (8-fold rosettes, girih tessellations), architectural arcs, minimalist vector motifs (open manuscripts, astrolabes, global network nodes), and refined Arabic calligraphic glyphs.
- **Atmospheric Quality**: Luminous, airy surfaces reminiscent of pristine vellum and quiet study sanctuaries, accented with celestial radiance.

## Colors

The palette balances weight, purpose, and celestial illumination:

- **Primary (`#0A1A3F` - Midnight Navy)**: Grounding and authoritative. Represents depth, timeless stability, and scholastic rigor. Used for high-emphasis headlines, structural foundations, primary interactive elements, and immersive footer planes.
- **Secondary (`#1E90FF` - Electric Blue)**: Vibrant and clear. Conveys modern connectivity, actionable progress, youth, and digital fluency. Used for functional highlights, progress indicators, interactive active states, and focused links.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37` - Celestial Gold)**: The hue of Noor, sacred heritage, and refined excellence. Reserved for honorific badges, completed certifications, subtle decorative borders, and sacred accents. Never over-saturated; applied with restraint.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFFFF` & `#F8FAFC` - Pure White & Alabaster Slate)**: Expansive, clean backgrounds that create open breathing room. 
- **Surface Borders (`#E2E8F0` - Muted Slate)**: Delicate boundaries preserving structure without visual friction.

Dark-mode or inverted sections use deep layers of Midnight Navy (`#0A1A3F` down to `#050D20`) with gold hairline borders and luminous electric blue focal points.

## Typography

The type system pairs modern geometric authority with academic legibility:

- **Headings (Space Grotesk)**: Chosen for its crisp, structural geometry and confident forward momentum, delivering impactful presence without feeling utilitarian.
- **Body & Functional UI (Inter)**: The standard for optical screen clarity. Renders dense curriculum outlines, course transcripts, and didactic long-form content with zero eye strain.
- **Arabic Inscriptions & Sacred Calligraphy**: Integrated as dedicated display treatments using traditional typography principles (such as Thuluth or Naskh typographic accents for "حق الدين"). Arabic passages must maintain an optical size 20–25% larger than accompanying Latin glyphs to match baseline visual weight, with generous line heights (1.8–2.0) to prevent diacritical collision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a responsive 12-column grid system bounded by a max container width of 1280px, maintaining editorial composure:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, generous 48px canvas margins. Vertical rhythm adheres to 40px, 80px, and 120px sectional cadence to cultivate contemplative calm.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px margins. Stacks content cards vertically while maintaining breathing room between learning modules.

The spatial rule emphasizes open negative space around headings, allowing sacred and intellectual passages to resonate without competitive density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on three-dimensional softness, pristine surface distinction, and subtle light refraction:

1. **Flat / Base (`Surface-0`)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` base plane.
2. **Elevated Low (`Surface-1`)**: Subtle lift for cards and feature panels.
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 26, 63, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(10, 26, 63, 0.02);`
   - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
3. **Elevated High / Interactive Hover (`Surface-2`)**: Dynamic upward lift for course card hover states.
   - `box-shadow: 0 20px 35px -8px rgba(10, 26, 63, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(10, 26, 63, 0.03);`
   - Border: 1px solid rgba(30, 144, 255, 0.25).
4. **Glassmorphism Backdrop (Sticky Header & Floating Triggers)**:
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`.
   - Backdrop filter: `blur(12px) saturate(180%)`.
   - Border bottom: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
5. **Pattern Watermarks**: Subtle vector girih patterns applied at 3% to 5% opacity in `#0A1A3F` or `#D4AF37`, sitting beneath content layers to provide cultural texture without compromising contrast.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **`rounded-2xl`** posture (16px / 1rem on standard cards and modules) reflecting warmth, accessibility, and modern polish:

- **Cards, Modals & Feature Panels**: 16px border-radius (`rounded-2xl`), softened to welcome the learner.
- **Pills & Status Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for high legibility and tactile delight.
- **Input Fields & Buttons**: 10px to 12px border radius for ergonomic comfort.
- **Arabesque Accent Badges**: Subtle scalloped or octagonal geometric framing for icons and category markers, honoring Islamic geometric proportion (such as an eight-pointed star container or 45-degree rotated squares with rounded vertices).

## Components

### 1. Sticky Glassmorphism Header
- **Layout**: Centered container with logo lockup (calligraphic seal + Latin wordmark), central pill-shaped navigation links, and a prominent free enrollment action.
- **Navigation Links**: Clean text with a subtle active pill background (`rgba(30, 144, 255, 0.08)` with `#1E90FF` typography).
- **Surface**: Translucent frosted glass with blurred background and slate hairline divider.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Midnight Navy background (`#0A1A3F`), crisp white text, 12px border radius. Hover introduces subtle lift and a celestial gold hairline rim (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(10, 26, 63, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary (Action)**: Electric Blue (`#1E90FF`), white text, designed for primary conversion steps (e.g., "Start Learning Free").
- **Tertiary / Outline**: Transparent background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#0A1A3F` text, transitioning to `#F8FAFC` on hover.

### 3. Course Cards
- **Structure**: Surface container (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-2xl` (16px), 1px slate border.
- **Header Element**: Abstract vector pattern illustration or calligraphic badge (NO photos).
- **Meta Chips**: Pill badge for topic track (e.g., "Public Speaking", "Aqeedah", "Leadership") paired with a "100% Free" Celestial Gold pill (`bg-[#D4AF37]/10 text-[#B89628]`).
- **Footer**: Lesson count, duration, and an arrow link with smooth micro-interaction.

### 4. Interactive FAQ Accordion
- **Rest State**: Clean horizontal card with `#F8FAFC` fill and `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Active State**: Shifts background to pure `#FFFFFF`, elevates with soft ambient shadow, illuminates the toggle icon with Electric Blue, and smoothly expands editorial typography.

### 5. Stat Strips & Feature Grids
- **Stat Strips**: Large geometric numerals (Space Grotesk) punctuated with Celestial Gold accents. Subtle grid separators with faint Islamic star vertex intersections.
- **Feature Cards**: Minimal vector icons (manuscript, speech soundwave, globe, astrolabe compass) housed in soft Electric Blue or Gold tinted circular-square containers.

### 6. Floating Contact & Support Button
- **Execution**: Circular or pill launcher anchored to the bottom-right viewport (24px margin).
- **Color**: WhatsApp Emerald (`#25D366`) paired with a high-contrast white glyph, elevated on a high-blur ambient shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`). Includes an optional expanding tooltip pill: "Questions? Ask our team".
